How it works: the merchant creates an invoice for an exact amount payable to a named payee. The payer looks the invoice up and sees the payee's name and the exact amount before approving anything. Payment pulls exactly that amount, exactly once — the InvoicePaid event is the receipt, verifiable by anyone on the explorer. Source & docs: see the project README.
